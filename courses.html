Core Subjects

Knowledge That Builds Bright Futures

Our curriculum focuses on the subjects that matter most for a strong academic foundation — taught with care, clarity, and a hands-on approach that keeps students curious and engaged.

Science

We make science come alive through practical learning, observation, and real conceptual understanding. Students explore how the world works through experiments and inquiry, building the curiosity and critical thinking that lead to genuine discovery.

  • Hands-on practical learning
  • Observation & experiments
  • Strong conceptual foundations

English

Our English program develops complete language ability — reading, writing, speaking, and listening. As an English-medium school, we help students communicate clearly and confidently, an essential skill for success in education and life.

  • Reading & writing fluency
  • Speaking & presentation
  • Confident communication skills

Computer Science

We prepare students for a digital world by teaching modern technology and computer basics. From essential skills to digital learning, students gain the confidence to use technology as a tool for creativity, study, and future opportunity.

  • Computer fundamentals
  • Modern technology skills
  • Digital learning tools

English Medium · Semester System

All subjects are taught in English and organized under a structured semester system. This approach keeps learning consistent and measurable, helping students stay on track, build strong language skills, and steadily progress toward academic excellence.

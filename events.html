Life at IMPS

Learning Beyond the Classroom

Our events bring students together to learn teamwork, creativity, and confidence in ways no textbook can. They celebrate effort, friendship, and the joy of growing up together as one school family.

Food Day

A favourite among students — children bring and share a variety of foods with their classmates. It's a joyful day that encourages creativity, generosity, and social bonding, helping students build friendships and a sense of community.

Annual Dinner

A highlight of the school year, our Annual Dinner brings students, staff, and families together to celebrate achievements. It is an evening of appreciation, warmth, and pride that strengthens the bond between school and home.

Sports Activities

Through sports, students develop physical fitness, discipline, and teamwork. Our activities encourage healthy competition and a spirit of cooperation, teaching valuable lessons about effort, fairness, and resilience.
